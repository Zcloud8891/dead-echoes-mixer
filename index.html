WHAT THIS IS · HONEST NOTE

A free browser soundboard for running a dark-fantasy table. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

  • Music: the six music layers are instrumental loops from the Dead Echoes Ambience album, generated with Suno AI from Dojo Studios' own prompts, then picked, trimmed, looped and loudness-matched by hand.
  • Synthesized: Rain and Wind & Stone are synthesized noise. The moment stingers (war drums, crit riser, victory fanfare, the low swell and bell) and the dice clicks are synthesized live in your browser.
  • Art: the pixel art is AI-assisted and selected by Dojo Studios.
  • Saving: My Mixes and the dice toggle live in this browser's local storage only.
